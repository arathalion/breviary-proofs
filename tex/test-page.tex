\office{Ordinary of the Divine Office at Lauds}
\begin{brevbody}
\hour{Lauds}
\vers{} O Lord, make haste to help me.

\resp{} O God, come to my assistance.

\begin{rubricpar}
Then the psalms and the canticle are said under one antiphon, or under five antiphons, as the Office of the day directs.

\end{rubricpar}
\brevrule
\begin{rubricpar}
This versicle \textcolor{black}{The Lord be with you} is not said by one who prays alone, nor by those who are not ordained deacons; but the following is said in its place:

\end{rubricpar}
\vers{} O Lord, hear my prayer.

\resp{} And let my cry come to you.

\psalmhead{95}
\ant{7}%
The Father is love, the Son is grace.

\opening[black]{S}{ing} to the Lord a new song \med{} sing to the Lord, all the earth. Sing to the Lord, and bless his name \med{} proclaim his help from day to day.

Tell his glory among the nations \med{} and his wonders among all the peoples. For the Lord is great \med{} and worthy of praise.

\brevrule
\lesson{vi}
\opening[black]{B}{y} this unity of nature, the Father is wholly in the Son, and the Son is wholly in the \unclear{}.

\heading{Way to say Preces}
Lord, have mercy. Christ, have mercy. Lord, have mercy. [Our Father] \rubric{in secret.} And lead us not into temptation.

\resp{} But deliver us from evil.
