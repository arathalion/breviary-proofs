\hour{Lauds}

\vers O Lord, make haste to help me.\\
\resp O God, come to my assistance.

\begin{rubricpar}
Then the psalms and the canticle are said under one antiphon, or under
five antiphons, as the Office of the day directs.
\end{rubricpar}

\brevrule

\rubric{This versicle} The Lord be with you \rubric{is not said by one
who prays alone, nor by those who are not ordained deacons; but the
following is said in its place:}

\vers O Lord, hear my prayer.\\
\resp And let my cry come to you.

\psalmhead{95}

\ant{7} The Father is love, the Son is grace.

\opening{S}{ing} to the Lord a new song\med sing to the Lord, all
the earth. Sing to the Lord, and bless his name\med proclaim his help
from day to day.

Tell his glory among the nations\med and his wonders among all the
peoples. For the Lord is great, and worthy of praise\med and to be
feared above all gods.

\brevrule

\lesson{vi}

\opening{B}{y} this unity of nature, the Father is wholly in the Son,
and the Son is wholly in the Father.

\begin{rubricpar}
After the versicle the following canticle is said, together with the
antiphon proper to the day.
\end{rubricpar}

\heading{Way to say Preces}

Lord, have mercy. Christ, have mercy. Lord, have mercy. \rubric{Our
Father} \rubric{in secret.} And lead us not into temptation.
\resp But deliver us from evil.
