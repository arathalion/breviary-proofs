#office Ordinary of the Divine Office at Lauds

.hour Lauds

.text
<V> O Lord, make haste to help me.
<R> O God, come to my assistance.

.rubric
Then the psalms and the canticle are said under one antiphon, or under five antiphons, as the Office of the day directs.

.rule

.rubric
This versicle {The Lord be with you} is not said by one who prays alone, nor by those who are not ordained deacons; but the following is said in its place:

.text

<R> And let my cry come to you.
